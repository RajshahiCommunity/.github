# RajshahiCommunity 🌇💻

**RajshahiCommunity** is a community initiative based in **Rajshahi, Bangladesh**, aimed at bringing together developers, designers, tech enthusiasts, and makers from the region. Our mission is simple: **Unite, Contribute, Collaborate, and Support** the local tech ecosystem.

---

## 🌟 Mission

To create a vibrant, inclusive, and collaborative tech community in Rajshahi where local talent can connect, grow, and contribute to the broader tech landscape.

---

## 🧩 What We Do

- 🤝 Connect Rajshahi-based developers and tech folks
- 💬 Host meetups, workshops, and tech talks
- 🌐 Build open source projects for and by the community
- 🚀 Support local startups and initiatives
- 🧠 Share knowledge and learning opportunities

---

## 🤔 Who Can Join?

Anyone from Rajshahi (or connected to Rajshahi!) who:

- Is passionate about tech
- Wants to learn, grow, and share
- Believes in community over competition
- Wants to contribute to open source and real-world projects

---

## 📌 How to Get Involved

1. **Join our Community Platforms** (coming soon!)
2. **Contribute to our Open Source Projects**
3. **Attend/Host a Local Event**
4. **Collaborate with Others**
